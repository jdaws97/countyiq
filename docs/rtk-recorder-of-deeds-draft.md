**Subject:** Right-to-Know Request — Recorded Document Index in Electronic Format

To the York County Open Records Officer:

Pursuant to the Pennsylvania Right-to-Know Law (Act 3 of 2008, 65 P.S. §§ 67.101–67.3104), I am requesting the following public records in electronic format:

**Records Requested:**

The recorded document index (NOT document images) maintained by the York County Recorder of Deeds, including the following data fields for all recorded instruments:

- Grantor name(s)
- Grantee name(s)
- Recording date
- Instrument type (deed, mortgage, lien, satisfaction, assignment, etc.)
- Book and page number
- Consideration amount (where recorded)
- Parcel identifier / property reference (where recorded)
- Municipality

**Date range:** All available records, or if that is impractical, records from January 1, 2015 to present.

**Preferred format:** CSV, Excel, or database export. Any standard electronic format is acceptable.

**Purpose:** This data will be used for a civic intelligence platform that aggregates public property data for York County. The data will be used for public information purposes — property research, ownership analysis, and civic transparency.

**Note:** I am requesting only the index/metadata of recorded documents — NOT copies of the document images themselves. This should significantly reduce the scope and cost of fulfilling this request.

I understand that the county may charge reasonable fees for electronic duplication. Please provide a fee estimate before proceeding if fees will exceed $20.

Please respond within five business days as required by the Right-to-Know Law. I am happy to discuss this request by phone or email to clarify the scope.
