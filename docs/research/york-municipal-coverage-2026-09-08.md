# York municipal coverage — September 8, 2026

All 72 incorporated municipalities reconciled with the YCPC boundary inventory. Configuration now covers 68 municipalities through 112 body sources (including one county-level source). This is configured coverage, not a claim that every record downloads successfully. See the companion recovery report for live retrieval results and remaining failures.

The companion CSV lists 383 USGS GNIS populated places and their governing municipality. All source IDs, names, coordinates, and point-in-polygon assignments were independently checked. A GNIS point does not define the entire community boundary, and informal names absent from GNIS are outside this catalog.
