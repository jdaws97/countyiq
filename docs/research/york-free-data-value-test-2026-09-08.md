# York free-data value test — September 8, 2026

The free sources justify a small ingestion pilot. They add dated permit activity, utility conditions, and reasons to downgrade a project. This review does **not** establish any currently available contract or sales opportunity.

We downloaded three official PDFs, extracted 38 commercial/occupancy row receipts from the first two pages of Springettsbury's report, and compared ten selected examples against the isolated CountyIQ research snapshot. The sample was chosen for useful content, not randomly; it cannot establish countywide yield or historical lead time. No RTK was filed, fees incurred, production data changed, or new evidence published to customers.

## What the source checks established

- All three YCCD permit identifiers above had zero matches in current raw proceedings in the local snapshot. That is evidence of additional identifiers, not proof they are absent from every CountyIQ table or document.
- The snapshot's current proceeding bodies include two Springettsbury Board documents and no body name containing “sewer.” Township discussion of a sewer authority is still present; missing authority-body coverage does not mean missing every utility fact.
- Springettsbury's application window ends July 31, but the report was generated August 10 and includes some August issuance dates. It is not a July 31 historical status snapshot.
- Several printed permit numbers are physically clipped and repeated across different rows. The extraction preserves the original PDF hash, page, coordinates, and printed values; those printed numbers must not become unique database keys. The 38 rows are a bounded extraction, not 38 distinct projects or fully validated leads. Page-edge continuations require review.
- The authority's [meeting index](https://nesewer.org/meeting-information/) exposed June 22 as its latest listed minutes in this check, with July agendas. No later decision was established. June conditions cannot be advertised as confirmed September conditions.
- YCCD says it posts its [review list weekly](https://www.yorkccd.org/erosion-and-sediment-control/pending-plan-review-list). Removal means a review completed; the resulting letter could describe deficiencies or approval. Disappearance must not become an approval event.

## Acquisition priority supported by this test

1. **YCCD review snapshots:** compact countywide coverage, usable permit identifiers, and a stated weekly cadence. Preserve review states and resolve site/phase differences before joining. A later snapshot comparison would test actual update yield.
2. **Springettsbury permit reports:** the strongest tested source for activity absent from the bounded existing searches. Start with commercial/occupancy sections, retain clipped values honestly, and resolve addresses before customer publication.
3. **Sewer-authority minutes:** enrich existing projects with specific constraints and decisions; also capture evidence that should lower a lead's priority. Source age and existing contractors matter.
4. **Targeted DEP decisions:** the [October 4, 2025 Pennsylvania Bulletin](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/secure/pabulletin/data/vol55/55-40/1356b.html) provides actual York permit decisions, including Core5 `PAD670033` in Manchester Borough. Existing planning also contains several Core5 projects elsewhere. Preserve the permit event separately until location establishes the match; no new parcel-linked DEP result is claimed here.

School procurement and grants remain useful parallel discovery work. The [free-data follow-up](york-free-data-followup-2026-09-08.md) records York Suburban's August 24 apparent-low-bid agenda and RACP evidence. An agenda motion needs later minutes or contract evidence before becoming an award. The [broader source inventory](york-public-data-discovery-2026-09-08.md) retains the other investigated public sources and gaps.
