# Bounded reviews of complete entity candidate components

Status: implementation candidate; live source review and release approval are
still required. The existing production backfill remains on its reviewed code.

The candidate graph's connected component remains the sole publication unit.
Large components can use temporary contexts of at most 40 names and 64,000
dynamic prompt characters through the subscription backend. Every candidate
edge is either covered by a context or has an unambiguous exact-registration
disposition. Registered-agent addresses do not establish common identity.

Each name has its own stable dossier, so one large owner's records cannot
consume another's detail allowance. Complete inventory hashes bind omitted
facts to source freshness. When details are truncated, the prompt also includes
every parcel PIDN grouped by mailing address and every tax-claim PIDN. This
exposes within-name differences outside the displayed detail. Index-only
identifiers cannot supply linked records. A dossier or complete comparison
that exceeds the context bound remains pending.

Every context uses source-validated N-trial voting with the existing adaptive
majority rule. Exact-registration shortcuts are validated against both the
registration index and complete dossier registration numbers. Units that merge
different proven registration numbers cannot vote.

The registration shortcut also requires corroboration for every attached
non-registration record. Each parcel's complete mailing address must match a
registration office after normalizing case, commas, periods, repeated spaces
and an optional ZIP+4 extension. Street tokens, unit designators, locality and
the five-digit ZIP remain significant. Every tax claim must use one of those
connected parcel identifiers. Licenses, proceedings, truncated portfolios and
unconnected records require source review. A registry identifier alone does
not identify the owner of a same-name parcel. Only fully corroborated groups
can remove candidate edges from model review.

This restriction also applies to ordinary small components. The production
source audit identified an earlier deterministic FF YORK / FFYORK assignment
that attached records at Wicklow Drive and Market Street without a source
bridge. The initial canary's structural acceptance did not establish this
cross-record identity. Existing deterministic results that do not meet the
corroboration rule require fresh source review and ordinary contradiction
handling before the next pass consumes the refreshed graph.

Overlapping positive proposals only define the scope of another review. When
membership, entity type, rejection or within-group uncertainty conflicts, all
names in that overlap receive a complete new review. No transitive identity
union is inferred. An overlap that cannot fit remains unfinished. Compatible
proposals retain their explicit membership, their minimum confidence and their
source-supported citations.

Relationships survive only when their complete original member group belongs
to one final entity and the target has a final identity. A split origin cannot
transfer a claim to one selected fragment. Superseded, internal and withheld
relations remain in private audit provenance with explicit reasons.

The final payload retains every validated unit vote, actual native receipts,
per-unit voting statistics and complete overlap reviews. It does not claim that
these were independent trials over the full parent. A fresh snapshot must match
the full parent source and candidate boundaries immediately before the ordinary
ledger gate and atomic graph replacement. Contradiction resolution uses the
same full-source collection method.

Planning policy is separate from the evidence hash. Changing context bounds or
the plan does not pretend that underlying evidence changed. Unit receipt keys
include the complete unit source hash and the review phase; reconciliation
cannot reuse an initial vote as a new vote. A configured per-run call cap has a
distinct resumable status. Authentication or quota failures still stop the run
without a paid fallback. Operators must require actual new-call progress before
restarting a run that completed no parents.

Local adversarial tests cover overlap disagreement, source changes, candidate
splits/joins, hidden mailing-address differences, missing reviews, registration
conflicts, relationship attribution, and partial-vote resumption. Production
quality identity includes the planner, reconciler, asset and contradiction
resolver. Passing structural tests alone is not source-quality approval.
